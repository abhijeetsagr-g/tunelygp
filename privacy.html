Privacy Policy for Tunely

Effective date: August 16, 2026

Tunely ("the App") is an offline music player developed by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Overview

Tunely is designed to work primarily offline. The App does not require account creation, does not collect analytics, and does not use advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. Your music library stays on your device.

2. Information We Access

Local Device Storage

Tunely requests permission to access media/storage on your device solely to read and play audio files already stored on your phone. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services Used

Tunely connects to the internet only for two specific, limited purposes:

Deezer API — used to fetch artist images. When you view an artist in the app, the artist's name is sent to Deezer to retrieve a matching image. No personal or device-identifying information is included in this request.

LRCLIB API — used to fetch song lyrics. When you search for lyrics, the song title and/or artist name is sent to LRCLIB to retrieve matching lyrics. No personal or device-identifying information is included in this request.

Neither service receives your name, contact details, device identifiers, or any data beyond the song/artist metadata needed to perform the lookup. We encourage you to review each provider's own privacy practices:

  • Deezer: https://www.deezer.com/legal/personal-datas
  • LRCLIB: https://lrclib.net

3. Data We Do Not Collect

Tunely does not:

  • Collect analytics or usage statistics
  • Use advertising SDKs
  • Use crash-reporting or telemetry SDKs
  • Require sign-in or collect account information
  • Sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes

4. Data Storage and Sharing

All music files, playback history, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal data, and we do not share data with third parties beyond the limited API calls described in Section 2.

5. Children's Privacy

Tunely is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

Since Tunely does not collect or store personal data on external servers, there is no personal data for us to delete or export on your behalf. If you have questions about specific data sent to Deezer or LRCLIB, please contact those providers directly, or reach out to us using the contact details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
